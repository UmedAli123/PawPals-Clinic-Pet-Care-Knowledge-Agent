PawPals Pet Clinic - Puppy House Training Tips

House training takes patience and a steady routine. Most puppies learn the basics in 4 to 6 months, but some small breeds take longer.

Set a routine
Take your puppy outside first thing in the morning, after every meal, after naps, after play and right before bed. Young puppies may need to go out every 1 to 2 hours.

Use one spot
Bring your puppy to the same outdoor spot each time. The familiar smell helps them understand what to do. Stay with them and use a calm cue word such as "go potty".

Reward right away
Give a small treat and praise within 3 seconds of finishing outside. Rewards given later will not connect to the behaviour.

Handle accidents calmly
Do not punish or scold your puppy for accidents. Clean the spot with an enzyme cleaner to remove the smell. If you catch your puppy in the act, calmly say "oops" and take them outside.

Crate training
A crate that is just big enough to stand and turn around in can help, because dogs avoid soiling their sleeping area. Do not leave a puppy in a crate longer than they can hold it. A rule of thumb is one hour for each month of age, plus one.

When to call the clinic
Frequent accidents, straining, blood in urine or a sudden change in habits can point to a medical problem rather than a training problem.
