PawPals Pet Clinic - Dog Vaccination Schedule

Purpose
This care sheet explains when puppies and adult dogs should receive core and non-core vaccines at PawPals Pet Clinic.

Puppy schedule
Puppies should receive their first vaccine at 6 to 8 weeks of age. Boosters are given every 3 to 4 weeks until the puppy is 16 weeks old. The core vaccine covers distemper, parvovirus, adenovirus and parainfluenza. The rabies vaccine is given at 12 to 16 weeks, as required by local law.

Adult dogs
Adult dogs need a core booster one year after the final puppy vaccine, then every 3 years. Rabies boosters follow the schedule printed on the rabies certificate, usually every 1 to 3 years.

Non-core vaccines
Bordetella (kennel cough), leptospirosis and Lyme vaccines are recommended based on lifestyle. Dogs that visit boarding kennels, groomers or dog parks should have the Bordetella vaccine at least 7 days before the visit.

After vaccination
Mild tiredness, a small lump at the injection site and a slight drop in appetite can last up to 48 hours. Call the clinic right away if your dog has facial swelling, repeated vomiting, hives or trouble breathing, because these can be signs of an allergic reaction.

Keep your pet's vaccination record in a safe place and bring it to every visit.
