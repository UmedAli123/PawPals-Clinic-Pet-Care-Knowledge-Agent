PawPals Pet Clinic - Flea and Tick Prevention

Fleas and ticks can cause itching, skin infections, anaemia and serious diseases. Prevention is easier than treatment.

Year-round protection
Use a veterinarian-approved preventive every month for the whole year. Fleas can survive indoors in cold weather, and ticks can be active on warm winter days.

Product options
Options include monthly spot-on treatments, chewable tablets and long-acting collars. Our vets will recommend a product based on your pet's species, weight and age.

Safety warning for cats
Never use dog flea products on cats. Some dog products contain permethrin, which is toxic to cats and can cause tremors or seizures. Contact the clinic immediately if this happens.

Checking your pet
After walks in grass or wooded areas, check your pet's head, ears, neck, armpits and between the toes for ticks. Remove a tick with fine-tipped tweezers by grasping it close to the skin and pulling straight out. Clean the area and keep the tick in a sealed container in case the vet wants to see it.

Treating the home
If you find fleas, wash pet bedding in hot water, vacuum carpets and furniture often, and treat all pets in the household at the same time. A full flea cycle can take 3 months to clear.

Signs of a problem
Contact the clinic if you see hair loss, red irritated skin, pale gums, or if your pet is lethargic after a tick bite.
